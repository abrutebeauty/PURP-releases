Privacy

COREKULT is a small sound pack shop run from Sweden. We keep what we collect to a minimum, we never sell it, and we never use it for ads.

Who to contact

Email hello@corekult.com to see, fix or delete anything we have about you, or with any question.

Always on (necessary)

Your cart, picked currency, cookie choice and the email you typed are kept in your own browser so the shop works. They never leave your device unless you send them.

When you give us your email

For free samples, a deal card or an order we keep your email, which packs it got, the country it came from and the date. We use it to deliver your packs, run your loyalty meter and deal card. We only send news if you tick a separate box. Kept until you ask us to delete it.

Stats (only after Allow)

A random visitor number, return visits, pages and packs opened, sample plays, add to cart and checkout, country and phone or computer. No names, no emails, no IP addresses. Stored by us on Cloudflare in the EU and deleted after 12 months. Tracking links (corekult.com/r/...) only tell us which link you came from.

Heatmaps and recordings (only after Allow)

Microsoft Clarity records clicks, scrolls and screen recordings so we can make the shop easier to use. Everything you type is blurred. Recordings are kept 30 days and heatmaps 13 months. Microsoft is the processor, see its privacy statement.

Payments

Payments run through Payhip, Stripe and PayPal. They handle your card or PayPal details, we never see them.

Your rights

You can change or take back your cookie choice any time with the button below or the Cookies link at the bottom of every page. You can ask to see, fix, move or delete your data, and you can complain to the Swedish privacy authority (IMY, imy.se). Under 13? Ask a parent before giving us your email.
